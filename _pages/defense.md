My PhD defense will take place on september 30th 2025, 14:00, in Amphi Turing, in [Sophie Germain building](https://u-paris.fr/batiment-sophie-germain/), 8 place Aurélie Nemours, 75013 Paris.

## The thesis

## About the defense
I will defend my thesis in front of a jury composed of:
- Jérôme Leroux, Reviewer
- Roland Meyer, Reviewer
- Nathalie Bertrand, Examiner
- Anca Muscholl, Examiner
- Sylvain Schmitz, Examiner (head of the jury)
- Arnaud Sangnier, Director
- Nathalie Sznajder, Co-supervisor

## Program
The presentation will last 45 minutes and will be followed by questions from the jury. The defense will be followed by a *pot de thèse* at IRIF, located at the 4th floor of the same building. 

## Access
The nearest public transportation stations are:
- Bibliothèque François Mittérand : Metro 14 and RER C
- Avenue de France : Tram T3a
- Porte de France : bus 89 and 62
  

The Amphi Turing is at floor -1 of the building, accessible through the stairs in front of the three lifts near the reception.
