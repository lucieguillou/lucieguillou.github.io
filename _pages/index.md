# About me 
I am an assistant professor (Maîtresse de Conférences) at Université Paris Cité, and a member of IRIF.


Before that, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the Max Planck Institute for Software Systems (MPI SWS) advised by Rupak Majumdar. 
Between october 2022 and september 2025, I was a PhD student at the Institut de Recherche en Informatique Fondamentale (IRIF), under the supervision of Arnaud Sangnier (DIBRIS) and Nathalie Sznajder (LIP6). I defended my PhD on september 30th 2025.

My research field is Verification of parameterized distributed systems.
