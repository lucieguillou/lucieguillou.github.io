About me

I am an assistant professor (Maîtresse de Conférences) at Université Paris Cité, and a member of IRIF.

Before that, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the Max Planck Institute for Software Systems (MPI-SWS) in Kaiserslautern, advised by Rupak Majumdar. Between october 2022 and september 2025, I was a PhD student at the Institut de Recherche en Informatique Fondamentale (IRIF), under the supervision of Arnaud Sangnier (DIBRIS) and Nathalie Sznajder (LIP6).

My research interests are verification of distributed systems, especially with an unbounded number of processes.
